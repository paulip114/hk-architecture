# Lifts and Escalators Ordinance (Cap. 618)

Draft working rules, limited to what affects architectural practice. Source: Cap. 618, version date 14.3.2024, read on 2026-11-07. No tool in this repo checks these rules, so a tool's PASS says nothing about them.

This ordinance controls lift and escalator safety and use. For design requirements, see the Barrier Free Design Manual Divisions 19 and 20 (`../bfa/bfa-rules.md`) and B(C)R ss.39-41 (`../bcr-bar/bcr-bar-rules.md`). Cap. 406 is the Electricity Ordinance, not this ordinance.

## Who is responsible
The **responsible person** is the owner, or any other person with management or control of the lift or escalator (s.2). For a new building, make clear who this is at handover.

## Lifts
| Requirement | Section | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who may do the work | s.8 | Only qualified or specified persons may personally carry out lift works |
| Before first use | s.20 | A registered lift engineer examines the lift with load and thoroughly examines its associated equipment |
| After major alterations | s.21 | Before normal use resumes, a registered lift engineer examines the whole lift or the affected part, as the section sets out |
| Use permit | s.26 | The Director of Electrical and Mechanical Services may issue a permit on application to put a lift into use or to continue its use |
| Permit validity | s.27 | A permit for putting a lift into use expires on the first anniversary of the date of completion of the examination |
| Periodic maintenance | s.15, Sch. 5 Part 1 | A registered lift contractor carries it out, within a one-month period from the last works |
| Periodic examination | s.22, Sch. 5 Part 2 | A registered lift engineer examines the lift within one year of the last examination |
| Displaying the permit | s.39 | For a lift with a car, other than a Schedule 4 lift, the current permit is displayed in a conspicuous position in the car |

## Programme note for architects
Allow for the engineer's examination and the use permit before lifts are needed for occupation. This is separate from the BA's occupation permit. The interaction with OP documents is not stated here, so confirm it with EMSD and the lift contractor.

## Escalators
Part 3 has a parallel structure (for example s.42 and s.44), and Schedule 5 also covers escalators. Its periods and examination details were not read for this document, so do not assume that the lift periods apply.

## Not covered here
Registration of contractors, engineers and workers; Part 3 details; orders and appeals (ss.30-37); incident reporting (ss.40-41); offences other than as noted; and lifts listed in Schedule 4. A registered lift engineer or contractor should confirm the requirements for a specific installation.
