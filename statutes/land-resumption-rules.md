# Land resumption and compensation

Draft working rules, limited to what affects architectural practice. Sources: Cap. 124 (version date 24.8.2025), the LandsD ex-gratia compensation pamphlet and LAO PN 10/2025, read on 2026-11-07. No tool in this repo checks these rules. This is not valuation or legal advice, so a client with a claim needs a surveyor and a lawyer.

## How to tell whether a scheme is affected
Check for a notice of proposed resumption or an order under Cap. 124 before advising on a site, and check planned NDAs and public works. In an NDA, private land is generally resumed unless a land exchange proceeds, as set out in `nt-land-exchange-rules.md`. Other ordinances also provide for resumption, and this guide does not cover them.

## Statutory process (Cap. 124)
| Step | Section | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proposal and objections | s.2A to s.2G | Notice of proposed resumption, with objections considered, before submission to the Chief Executive in Council |
| Order | s.3, s.4 | Authorization and order for resumption, with notices |
| Purchase by agreement | s.4A | The Authority may agree a purchase before the land reverts, including reasonable professional costs |
| Reversion | s.5 | Ownership reverts to the Government |
| Offer or call for claims | s.6(1) | Within 28 days of reversion, the Authority makes an offer or requires a claim to be submitted |
| Claim by others | s.8 | Within 1 year of reversion, unless the period is extended. If the amount is not agreed, either party may go to the Lands Tribunal |
| Provisional payment | s.16A | The Authority may pay provisionally, with interest, if an offer is not accepted |

## How statutory compensation is assessed
- **Basis (s.10):** the loss or damage from the resumption, including the value of the land and buildings at the date of resumption, easements, severance and other items the section lists.
- **Later works (s.11):** the Tribunal may decline compensation for additions or improvements made after the notice is gazetted, unless they were needed for repair. So advise a client before spending on an affected site.
- **Exclusions (s.12):** no extra allowance because resumption is compulsory, no account of certain planning reservations, no compensation for a use that breaches the lease, and none for the expectancy of a grant or renewal. Section 12 has further rules.

## Ex-gratia compensation
Ex-gratia allowances are non-statutory. The Government may offer eligible landowners ex-gratia land compensation as an alternative to a statutory claim, and offers allowances and rehousing to eligible occupants.

PN 10/2025 gives Tier One and Tier Two zone rates for agricultural land in the New Territories of **$1,008 and $504 per square foot from 1 October 2025**. Rates change from time to time, and this figure may be out of date, so check the current LandsD rate before quoting one.

## Not covered here
Other resumption ordinances, business and occupant allowance rates, rehousing eligibility, building land rates, valuation method and interest rates.
