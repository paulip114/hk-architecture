# Railway protection (PNAP APP-24, Cap. 519, Cap. 276)

Draft working rules. Sources read on 2026-11-07: PNAP APP-24, December 2022 revision; Railways Ordinance (Cap. 519), version 1.9.2023, s.24 and s.27; Mass Transit Railway (Land Resumption and Related Provisions) Ordinance (Cap. 276), version 12.8.2021, s.15. No tool in this repo checks these rules, so a tool's PASS says nothing about them.

## How to tell whether a site is affected
1. **Railway protection area:** as a general guide, about 30 m outside the outer surface of railway structures and facilities or the railway fence or wall, or from the nearest rail if there is no fence or wall. It may take in the whole of any lot partly within that distance, and can be larger at stations (para 3).
2. **Confirm on the plans:** MTRCL holds the plans, viewable by appointment, and publishes information on its [protection boundary page](https://www.mtr.com.hk/en/corporate/operations/protection_boundary.html) (para 2).
3. **Area Number 3 of the scheduled areas:** some protection areas are also designated under BO Schedule 5, which adds controls. Check [BD's scheduled areas page](https://www.bd.gov.hk/en/resources/codes-and-references/scheduled-areas/index.html) (para 4).
4. **Outside the area:** the AP, RSE and RGE must still ensure that works do not harm railway structures or facilities (para 5).

APP-24 Appendix A lists 12 lines in operation: Airport Express, Disneyland Resort, East Rail, High Speed Rail, Island, Kwun Tong, Light Rail, South Island, Tseung Kwan O, Tsuen Wan, Tuen Ma and Tung Chung.

## Process (APP-24)
- **Plans:** BD circulates plans for works in the area to MTRCL. Cap. 519 s.27 or Cap. 276 s.15 will be invoked to require protective measures. Under those sections the BA may refuse approval or consent where works would be incompatible with the railway (para 6).
- **Monitoring plan:** include one in the plans for site formation, foundation, ELS and other works that could cause ground movement or vibration affecting the railway (para 7).
- **During works:** tell MTRCL directly when works commence. Send monitoring records every two weeks, or as agreed with MTRCL, and keep in regular contact (paras 7-8). MTRCL staff may be authorised to enter the site (para 9).
- **Suspension:** if a checkpoint reaches the pre-set limit, BD, EMSD and MTRCL notify each other at once. BD and EMSD issue a joint press release within 48 hours of the suspension, and again after resumption is agreed (paras 11-12). In principle, works affecting the railway stop once the "Action" limit is reached.
- **Ground investigation and underground drainage:** in Area Number 3, ground investigation needs prior approval and consent, and underground drainage works are not exempt, apart from works allowed under the minor works system. Elsewhere in the protection area, send proposals to MTRCL for comment before starting (paras 14-15).
- **Minor works:** for Class I and II works involving spread footings, excavation or underground drainage in Area Number 3, inform MTRCL and obtain its agreement before commencement (para 16).

## Design limits (APP-24 Appendix B)
| Item | Limit | Exception or note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Piles, foundations, boreholes, wells, soil nails, drains, rock bolts and similar | Not within 3 m of any point of underground railway structures and facilities | Unless MTRCL has given prior special agreement |
| Piling | Not within 3 m on plan of the railway fence or wall, or 7 m on plan of the nearest track centreline if there is no fence or wall | Unless MTRCL has given prior special agreement |
| Anchors, if allowed | More than 3 m from any railway structure or facility; centroid of the fixed length more than twice the fixed length away | |
| Pressure change on underground structures and facilities | Not more than 20 kPa, vertical or horizontal | May be reviewed case by case if MTRCL and BD accept an engineering approach |
| Openings near railway ventilation openings | Not closer than 5 m | May be 2.5 m if railway exhaust is directed away and is unlikely to affect the opening |
| Vertical greening near railway ventilation openings | Not closer than 5 m | |
| Scaffolding, platforms, signs and projections above track level | Not within 6 m on plan of the tracks | Unless MTRCL agrees, with protective measures |

Boreholes need a method statement for checking verticality where they are within 10 m on plan of underground railway structures and sunk to within 3 m of the highest point of those structures, and for depth control within 3 m on plan. Appendix B also covers demolition, falling object protection (PNAP APP-102), flood protection, marine works and wells.

## Not covered here
