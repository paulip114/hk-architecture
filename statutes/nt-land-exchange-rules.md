# New Territories land exchange

Draft working rules, limited to what affects architectural practice. Sources: LAO PN 2/2023, 11/2023, 11/2023A, 13/2023, 7/2026 and 10/2025, read on 2026-11-07. No tool in this repo checks these rules, and `tools/planning_lease_check.py` does not apply these standard rates. Read `../lands/lands-rules.md` first for the general procedure.

## How to tell which arrangement applies
| Situation | Arrangement | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small house grant | Not covered by PN 2/2023. See `small-house-rules.md` | PN 2/2023, `small-house-rules.md` |
| Site in a New Development Area earmarked for private development | Enhanced Conventional New Town Approach | PN 13/2023 and the notes for each area |
| Agricultural land outside an NDA, in the districts the pilot covers | Pilot scheme for premium at standard rates | PN 11/2023, 11/2023A and 7/2026 |
| Other land exchange | General procedure, with premium assessed for the case | PN 2/2023 |

## General procedure (PN 2/2023)
Only an applicant who owns all the lots to be surrendered, directly or through wholly-owned subsidiaries, or who is a prospective purchaser under a registered agreement with the owner's written consent, is accepted. LandsD checks title before making an offer.

## New Development Areas (PN 13/2023)
The Government, as a general principle, resumes and clears the private land planned for development in an NDA. Before it does, it allows land exchange from owners for sites earmarked for private development, if they meet the criteria and conditions. If the exchange falls through, the land is resumed and disposed of by tender or other means. Application windows differ by area, so read the note for that area.

PN 10/2025 lets an applicant offer other private land planned for resumption to offset the premium, in NDAs of the Northern Metropolis.

## Pilot scheme outside NDAs (PN 7/2026)
From 1 April 2026, standard rates apply by zone and by use before and after the exchange. PN 7/2026 replaces PN 4/2025. As an example, the rates for Yuen Long Town are:

| Use | HK$/m² |
| --- | --- |
| Before: agricultural | 5,000 |
| Before: non-residential | 11,500 |
| Before: residential | 23,500 |
| After: non-residential | 17,000 |
| After: residential | 31,000 |

Other zones have other rates, the higher rate applies where zones overlap, and rates are reviewed. Use the current note, and read PN 11/2023 and 11/2023A for eligibility and how premium is calculated.

## Sequence with planning
A land exchange does not replace planning permission. Check the OZP first, as set out in `../planning/planning-rules.md`.

## Not covered here
Eligibility and thresholds in the pilot scheme, the premium formula, the note for each NDA, and how to assemble land.
