# New Territories Small House Policy

Draft working rules, limited to what affects architectural practice. Sources: LandsD pamphlet *How to Apply for a Small House Grant*, Cap. 121 (version date 2.8.2012), and LAO PN 1/2025 and 1/2025B, read on 2026-11-07. No tool in this repo checks these rules. The pamphlet says it is a general guide, creates no legal rights and may be revised, so confirm details with the District Lands Office.

## How to tell whether a scheme is affected
The policy applies if the client is an indigenous villager building a village house under a small house grant. All of these must be met:
- The applicant is a male at least 18 years old, descended through the male line from a 1898 resident of a recognized village.
- The applicant has not already received a small house grant.
- The site is within the "V" zone and the environs or village extension area of a recognized village.
- The site is not affected by impending development and does not prejudice planning proposals.
- There are no substantiated local objections.

## Grant types
A small house grant can be a private treaty grant on Government land, a building licence on private land, or an exchange. Read the document actually issued, because its conditions apply.

## Building limits
A New Territories Exempted House does not go through the normal Buildings Department plan process, if it meets the conditions in the Cap. 121 Schedule. Under Part 1 of the Schedule:
- Not more than 3 storeys.
- A building over 7.62 m and up to 8.23 m high has a roofed-over area of no more than 65.03 m². Wall thickness conditions also apply.

The Schedule has further conditions, including other height and area combinations. Read it before relying on the figures above.

## Procedure
| Step | Who | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Application | Applicant to the District Lands Office | Composite form, with a declaration of status |
| Start of processing | DLO | Waiting time for new applications should not exceed one year |
| Interview and statutory declaration | Applicant | Bring evidence of status. A 1:1000 survey plan with the house, balcony, septic tank and soakaway helps |
| Public notice | DLO | Posted for 14 days in the village and Rural Committee |
| Offer | DLO | Offer letter with a demand note for the fee or premium |
| Grant | Applicant and DLO | Straightforward cases may take 24 weeks from the interview to execution |
| Certificates of Exemption | Applicant to the DLO | Needed for building, site formation and drainage works before work starts |
| Setting out | DLO | Applied for at the same time |

If Certificates of Exemption for site formation and drainage are refused because of site constraints, those plans must go to the Building Authority for approval first.

## Completion
LAO PN 1/2025 and 1/2025B set a self-certification arrangement for certificates of compliance and "No Objection to Occupy" letters, in suitable cases. Check whether it applies to the lot.

## Not covered here
Premium, alienation restrictions, the local objection mechanism, the list of recognized villages, the conditions in full, and any legal challenge to the policy.
