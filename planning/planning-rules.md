## 1. The statutory plans
| Plan | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Outline Zoning Plan (OZP, 分區計劃大綱圖) | Zones, Notes (use and development restrictions) and Explanatory Statement. Plan number `S/<area>/<n>`. Draft OZPs carry the same weight in practice once exhibited. |
| Development Permission Area plan (DPA, 發展審批地區圖) | Interim plan in the New Territories; enforcement against unauthorised development applies. |
| Regulated Area (新增, 2023) | Area on an OZP not previously under a DPA where the Secretary for Development can apply enforcement; development is an offence unless it is a pre-9.12.2022 use, always permitted, or has permission (new s21F). |
| Outline Development Plan / Layout Plan (ODP / LP) | Departmental plans, not statutory; they guide land sale and lease conditions. |
| HKPSG (香港規劃標準與準則) | Not statutory; sets reference densities and provision standards. Densities in `bpr/bpr-rules.md` §3. |

Read the **Notes** and **Explanatory Statement (ES)** together. The ES states the planning intention, which TPB uses to judge s16 and minor relaxation applications. Look up the plan on the Statutory Planning Portal (ozp.tpb.gov.hk) and check you have the latest gazetted version, including any draft amendment exhibited under s5 or s7.

## 2. How the Notes work
Covering Notes (common to all OZPs, from the TPB Master Schedule of Notes; urban version read):
- **Column 1 (第一欄)** – uses always permitted. **Column 2 (第二欄)** – uses that may be permitted by TPB, with or without conditions, on application under **s16**. A use in neither column is not permitted: it needs a plan amendment (s12A) unless it is an existing use.
- **Existing use (現有用途)**: no action is needed until there is a material change of use or redevelopment. Redevelopment must then comply (or get permission). An "existing building" is one that physically exists and complies with the relevant legislation and lease.
- **Temporary uses** expected to last **5 years or less** are always permitted in urban areas if they comply with other legislation and the lease (rural OZPs differ: usually temporary use up to 3 years needs permission unless in Column 1) [verify per plan].
- Always permitted in all zones (unless stated): open space, roads, utilities, geotechnical, drainage and waterworks works, and uses directly related and ancillary to a permitted use.
- Permitted uses must still comply with **lease conditions** and other legislation (Covering Notes para 2). Planning approval does not change the lease.
- Minor boundary adjustment of zones and roads is allowed as detailed planning proceeds.

Remarks (說明) of each zone carry the development restrictions. Typical R(A) wording (from A/K10/280, Ma Tau Kok OZP S/K10/31):
> "... maximum plot ratio of 7.5 for a domestic building or 9.0 for a building that is partly domestic and partly non-domestic, of which the domestic part shall not exceed 7.5, or the plot ratio of the existing building, whichever is the greater ... maximum building height of 120 mPD, or the height of the existing building, whichever is the greater ... minor relaxation of the PR and BH restrictions may be considered by the Board on application under section 16."

Other common Remarks: GFA to be disregarded (plant rooms, caretaker's office and quarters, car parks, recreational facilities for residents) "in determining the maximum PR"; non-building areas (NBA, 非建築用地), building gaps and setbacks shown on the plan; site coverage caps; number of storeys. Always read the Remarks of the specific zone on the specific plan.

## 3. Plot ratio, GFA and height under the OZP (JPN 4, JPN 5)
- **JPN 4 (Development Control Parameters – PR/GFA, effective 18.10.2021)**: the stricter of the OZP and B(P)R applies. For plans gazetted on or after 18.10.2021 the OZP PR/GFA is the sole reference except in exceptional cases. Where B(P)R is stricter, the BA may grant a **s42 BO modification** to reach the OZP figure.
- **BD is the sole agent** that verifies GFA. PlanD and LandsD use BD's verified GFA and BD's concession practice (PNAP APP-2, APP-151) unless the plan or a planning approval says otherwise.
- **JPN 5 (Building Height Restriction, effective 15.5.2019)**: BHR is set in mPD (or metres / storeys) on the OZP; leases normally do not repeat it.
  - Measured to the **top of the main roof** (roof over the highest usable floor space). Where in metres, from the **mean site formation level** (average of highest and lowest formation levels).
  - **Roof-top structures not counted**, unless (a) enclosed covered structures exceed **50% of the roof area** below, or (b) they exceed these heights above the main roof:
    | Structure | Building ≤ 30 m (≤ 35 m for features) | Taller building |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Ancillary plant, tanks, lift machine room | 3 m (5.2 m for lift) | lesser of 10% of building height or 15 m |
    | Architectural features – residential / composite | lesser of 15% of height or 7 m | same |
    | Architectural features – other buildings | 7 m | 20% of height, plus a further 20% for a spire |
    | Signboards | as ancillary, without the lift allowance | as ancillary |
  - Basements count as storeys unless the plan says otherwise; storey height and transfer plate per PNAP APP-5.
- **JPN 7** (Site Coverage Restriction, Aug 2021) – SC on OZP, not read in this session [verify].
- **JPN 6** (SBD building separation and setback) – covered in the APP-152 thread.

## 4. Applications to TPB
| Route | When | Time limit (post-1.9.2023) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **s12A** – amendment of plan (改劃) | Use not in Column 1/2, or restrictions far beyond "minor relaxation" | TPB considers within **2 months** (was 3). Only owners, persons with owner's or Director of Lands' consent, or public bodies may apply; others go to an administrative register. |
| **s16** – planning permission (規劃許可) | Column 2 use; minor relaxation of PR / BH / SC / NBA where the Remarks allow; CDA MLP; transfer of plot ratio (PG-43A) | TPB considers within **2 months of receipt** (GN para 46). |
| **s16A** – amendment to permission | Class A amendments: no application needed. Class B: apply under s16A(2) (PG-36C). Others: fresh s16 | [verify period] |
| **s17** – review (覆核) | Applicant dissatisfied with s16 / s16A decision | Apply in writing **within 21 days** of notification, with grounds; TPB considers within **3 months** (GN paras 50, 53) |
| **s17B** – appeal to Town Planning Appeal Board | After a review | Lodge **within 60 days** of notification of the review decision (GN para 58) |

s16 procedure (TPB Guidance Notes, January 2026):
1. **Anyone** may apply; owner's consent or notification is required if the applicant is not the sole "current land owner" (owner as at 6 weeks before application; consent / notice normally within 1 year before) – PG-31B.
2. Forms: S16-I (general), S16-II (NTEH only), S16-III (rural temporary ≤ 3 years). Plans and a supporting planning statement are optional but in practice needed; any supplementary information needs a bilingual executive summary ≤ 500 words. Non-EPASS: 1 signed form + 4 hard copies + 1 soft copy.
3. Published for public inspection; **comments within the first 3 weeks** (PG-30D).
4. **Further information (FI)**: only within 2 months of receipt, or within 2 months after a deferment for FI; one batch; must not materially change the nature of the application. FI may be exempted from publication.
5. **Deferment** request must reach the Secretary before the paper is issued (normally 7 days before the meeting) – PG-33B.
6. Decision by the Metro Planning Committee (MPC) or Rural and New Town Planning Committee (RNTPC). Written notice about 2 weeks after the meeting. Approval conditions must be complied with (PG-20A).
7. **Validity**: permission lapses if development has not commenced by the stated date – typically **4 years** for permanent development (A/K10/280: meeting 24.7.2026, valid to 24.7.2030); extension under PG-35E; lapsing under PG-26A. Temporary permissions: usually 3 years (rural) or ≤ 5 years (urban); renewal 2–4 months before expiry (PG-34D).
8. No application fee is stated in the Guidance Notes.

### What TPB weighs in a minor relaxation (from A/K10/280 and PG-41A)
Planning intention in the ES, planning and design merits (setbacks, public passage, greening, GIC facilities), visual impact (VIA to PG-41A), air ventilation (AVA), traffic, environmental, sewerage and drainage impacts, previous and similar applications, and public comments. A typical paper: proposal → justifications → owner's consent → background and similar applications → departmental comments → public comments → planning assessment → PlanD view with approval conditions and advisory clauses.

## 5. Current incentive schemes (2026)
- **Bonus Plot Ratio Pilot Scheme (JPN 9, 1.9.2026 to 31.8.2031)**: seven areas – Cheung Sha Wan, Ma Tau Kok, Mong Kok, Sai Ying Pun & Sheung Wan, Tsuen Wan, Wan Chai, Yau Ma Tei. Eligible: site ≥ 700 m²; existing residential building(s) with OP issued ≥ 50 years before the application; redevelopment > 50% residential GFA. Bonus = **20% of the maximum domestic PR/GFA on the OZP**, land-premium free (standard rate per m² GFA set by LandsD, LAO PN 9/2026 – see `lands/lands-rules.md`). Option (i) build in situ (needs s16 if the OZP limits are exceeded; s42 BO if B(P)R exceeded) or option (ii) encash as premium credit; no hybrid.
- **Transfer of Plot Ratio (TPB PG-43A, revised 21.8.2026)**: s16 application; sending sites in the same seven areas; receiving sites anywhere in HK, ≥ 700 m² (was 1,000 m²), up to 30% of the receiving site's maximum GFA (minor exceedance allowed), no limit on number of receiving sites. Can be combined with the Bonus PR scheme.

## 7. Workflow for a scheme
1. Find the OZP, zone, Notes, ES, NBA / building gap / setback annotations and any draft amendment.
2. Check the proposed uses against Column 1 / Column 2.
3. Check PR (domestic, non-domestic, total), BH (mPD, with JPN 5 roof-top rules), SC and NBA against the Remarks.
4. Decide the route: none / s16 (Column 2 use or minor relaxation) / s12A.
5. Run `tools/planning_lease_check.py` for the lease side at the same time – a lease modification normally follows planning permission.
6. Build the programme: s16 ≈ 2 months to decision + ~2 weeks for the notice; add 3–4 months if a review is likely.
