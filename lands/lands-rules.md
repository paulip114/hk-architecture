# Land administration (Lands Department) – lease conditions, lease modification and premium

Read this with `planning/planning-rules.md` before any lease check, lease modification, land exchange or premium question. Every output is a draft for review by the Authorized Person, the client's solicitor and surveyor. A Government lease is a **contract**: LandsD acts as landlord, at its absolute discretion, not as a statutory authority. Items marked **[verify]** were not checked against a primary source in this session.

Last compiled: 2026-11-05. Sources read: LAO PN 2/2023, 4/2021, 2/2018, 4/2014A, 1/2004, 2/2026, 9/2026; LAO PN index (page revised 6.10.2026); JPN 4, 5, 9; LegCo brief on standard-rate premium (Mar 2022); SDEV reply on premium processing times (26.3.2025). Links at the end.

## 1. Lease documents
| Document | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Government (formerly Crown) lease (官契) | Old leases, often short: user clause like "not to use as offensive trade" only, or no GFA cap ("virtually unrestricted lease", LAO PN 1/2017) |
| Conditions of Sale / Grant / Exchange (賣地／批地／換地條件) | Modern leases: full development conditions |
| Modification Letter (修訂契約函件) | Varies an existing lease; registered at the Land Registry |
| Waiver letter (豁免書) | Temporary relaxation of user etc. while the lease stays unchanged; fee payable; can be terminated |
| Consent / approval letter | Under an existing discretionary clause of the lease (PN 4/2021) |

Lease term: most leases expiring before 2047 were extended to 30.6.2047 and beyond under the Basic Law and the New Territories Leases (Extension) Ordinance (Cap. 150), with Government rent at 3% of rateable value (Cap. 515) [verify]. Check the lot's lease from the Land Registry search (IL / KIL / NKIL / STTL / TPTL / DD lot number).

## 2. Clauses that drive design
Typical clauses an AP must check (LAO PN 2/2018 Appendix I lists them for the Development Schedule):
- **User (用途)** and type of building – e.g. "non-industrial (excluding godown, hotel and petrol filling station)", "private residential". A use outside the user clause needs a waiver or lease modification, even if the OZP permits it.
- **Maximum / minimum GFA (最高／最低總樓面面積)** – modern leases mirror the OZP PR (JPN 4 para 2). A minimum GFA clause can also bind.
- **Site coverage (上蓋面積)** – SC beyond the lease limit for PNAP APP-2 / APP-151 features needs a discretionary clause; where it is missing, lease modification at administrative fee, then premium for the relaxation (PN 1/2004).
- **Height** – generally no longer in new leases (JPN 5 para 4); in old leases an mPD or storey limit may remain and needs modification to remove.
- **Design, Disposition and Height (DDH, 設計、佈局及高度)** – Director's approval of the layout; no premium for approval itself (PN 4/2021 App A(I)); PN 3/2020 and 3/2020A explain the clause [text was scanned, not read – verify].
- **Building covenant (建築規約)** – complete a minimum GFA within a stated period (often 48–72 months) and obtain the Certificate of Compliance (滿意紙, PN 1/2026); extensions under PN 2/2021, 2/2022, with premium.
- **Non-building area, setback, building separation, greenery / landscape (PN 1/2020), tree preservation (PN 6/2023)**.
- **Car parking, loading / unloading, vehicular access points** – rates may differ from HKPSG; variation usually attracts premium (PN 4/2021 App B).
- **GFA exemption clauses** – see §3.
- **Master Layout Plan (MLP)** for large lots (PN 1/1991, 3/1994, 6/1996).
- **Alienation restrictions**, Government accommodation / GIC facilities, public open space, footbridge connections.

## 3. GFA under the lease (JPN 4, LAO PN 4/2014A)
LandsD checks the lease maximum GFA using BD's verified GFA. Concessions are in three groups:
| Group | Examples | Under the lease |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Open flat roofs, open private car parks, refuge floors, larger lift shafts, mandatory plant rooms | Not counted by any department, whatever the lease says, if PNAP criteria are met |
| II (nil premium) | Caretaker's quarters, certain green / innovative features, OC office | Exempt only if the lease has a **GFA exemption clause**; nil premium, administrative fee |
| II (premium) | Curtain walls, balconies, utility platforms, non-structural prefabricated external walls, noise barriers, MiC, residential recreational facilities | Exempt only with a clause **and premium** – standard rates for balconies, UPs and external walls (PN 3/2026), MiC (PN 4/2026) |
| III | PTI, Government accommodation, covered public passage, covered private car parks | Case by case, specified in the lease |
If an existing lease lacks the clause, a **lease modification** is needed, premium case by case. Above-ground car park exemption: PN 9/2025. Use-specific GFA (retail, office, welfare): LandsD verifies itself (PN 3/2018). BIM area calculations: PN 6/2024.

## 4. Building plans under the lease (PN 2/2018, 4/2018)
- GBP go through BD's Centralised Processing System; LandsD comments as landlord. The AP submits a **Development Schedule** confirming each lease clause with drawing references, and separate GFA / SC calculations where they differ from the BO figures. Non-compliant submissions are rejected without scrutiny.
- LandsD's objection does not stop BD approval under the BO, but building against the lease is a breach – re-entry risk. Pre-submission enquiries and workshops with the District Lands Office's Building Plan Unit (PN 4/2018).
- Plans may be processed before the lease modification is executed (PN 2/2002) [RTF, not read – verify].

## 5. Routes when the scheme breaches the lease
| Route | Use when | Premium | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Approval under an existing clause (PN 4/2021 App A(I)) | Clause already allows the Director to approve (DDH, landscape, MLP) | None | During GBP checking |
| Relaxation, no premium (PN 4/2021 App A(II)) | Vehicular access variation, noise barriers, building separation, disabled parking, minor structures in NBA / setback | None (if departments agree) | During GBP checking |
| Approval / consent with premium (PN 4/2021 App B, fast track) | Setback relaxation, car park GFA exclusion, parking provision variation, MLP amendment | Yes + admin fee | Premium advised within **16 weeks** of a valid application |
| Waiver (豁免書) | Temporary change of use of existing building (e.g. industrial premises, PN 5/2026; special waiver for whole IB conversion, PN 8/2024) | Waiver fee | |
| **Lease modification (契約修訂)** | Permanent change of user, GFA, height, SC beyond the clauses | Full market premium (or standard rates where a scheme applies) | See §6 |
| **Land exchange (換地)** | Amalgamating lots, surrendering land and regranting, or large changes; new lease issued | As above | See §6 |
| Private treaty grant (私人協約批地) | Government land to a specific party on policy support | Full market premium | |

Typical sequence: **planning permission (s16) or rezoning (s12A) first, then lease modification**, then GBP approval and consent to commence (PN 2/2023 App II asks for the TPB approval letter "if applicable"). For the Bonus PR pilot, s16 must be obtained before the lease modification application (JPN 9 para 3).

## 6. Lease modification / land exchange procedure (LAO PN 2/2023, 31.3.2023)
1. **Application** by the registered owner, a purchaser under a registered ASP with owner's consent, or an authorised agent. Submit: application letter, 1:1000 location plan, TPB approval letter (if applicable), Land Registry printout certified within 1 month, authorisation / consent, personal data consent. Helpful: OZP extract, sketch plans, impact assessments.
2. **Preliminary reply** within **3 weeks**: whether the case can proceed; initial administrative fee (and LACO fee for land exchange) payable within about **2 weeks**, else deemed withdrawn.
3. Title checking (land exchange); circulation to departments.
4. **District Lands Conference (DLC)** decision; applicant may attend or make written representations. Outcome within **22 weeks of the preliminary reply**: rejection, agreement in principle subject to issues / statutory procedures, or a **Provisional Basic Terms Offer (PBTO)** without premium.
5. Accept PBTO within **2 weeks**; premium information may be submitted within 2 weeks. No negotiation.
6. **Binding Basic Terms Offer (BBTO)** with premium. Accept within **4 weeks**: acceptance letter, balance fees, **10% premium deposit**, mortgagee's no-objection. Acceptance date = Contract Date (binding).
7. Within **8 weeks** of the Contract Date: title matters, mortgage release draft. Within **12 weeks**: execute the document, pay the balance of premium and Land Registry fees.
8. **Premium appeal**: return the Appeal Letter within the BBTO period (other terms accepted, fees paid). The BBTO premium lapses and the revised premium is at the then market value (can go up). Counter-offer with justification within **3 months**; decided by the Valuation Conference. Fast track for 2nd and 3rd appeals: counter-offer within 12 working days, revised BBTO within 24 working days. If the revised premium equals the counter-offer and is not accepted, the case is deemed withdrawn.
9. Nil or nominal premium cases: one offer only.

Observed premium negotiation time (SDEV, 26.3.2025): average 8.5 months (2022), 5.8 months (2023), 7.8 months (2024). Lease processing can start in parallel with a s12A that has defined parameters.

Programme rule of thumb (uninterrupted case, no appeal): 3 weeks + 22 weeks + 2 weeks + premium assessment (~6–8 months) + 4 weeks + 12 weeks ≈ **16–18 months** from application to execution. Add 3+ months per premium appeal.

## 7. Premium (補地價)
- **Premium = After Value − Before Value** (− deductible for Government works the lessee builds, where accepted). Before = land value under the existing lease; After = under the modified lease. Full market value, assessed by LandsD valuers (LegCo brief, Mar 2022).
- **Standard rates** (標準金額) replace case-by-case valuation in set schemes: NDAs (PN 1/2022, 3/2022, 5/2024, 13/2023), redevelopment of pre-1987 industrial buildings (PN 6/2026), NT agricultural land (PN 7/2026), MiC (PN 4/2026), balconies / UPs / external walls (PN 3/2026), Bonus PR pilot (PN 9/2026). Premium = rate × GFA difference (or site area for agricultural land). Choice of standard rates is optional and irreversible once assessment starts.
- **Bonus PR standard rates (PN 9/2026, from 1.9.2026, per m² of bonus GFA)**: Sai Ying Pun & Sheung Wan $84,000; Wan Chai $84,000; Ma Tau Kok $66,000; Cheung Sha Wan $63,000; Mong Kok $70,000; Yau Ma Tei $70,000; Tsuen Wan $46,000. Reviewed annually; no appeal.
- **"Pay for What You Build" pilot (PN 2/2026)**: applications 1.6.2026–31.5.2029, non-residential zones only. Premium on an initial phase of ≥ 60% of the maximum GFA; the balance can be taken up by further modification within 10 years of the first OP, at the then market value.
- Administrative fees are separate and reviewed yearly (PN 1/2004 quoted $23,000 in 2004 – not current).

## 8. Workflow for a scheme
1. Get the lease (all modification letters too) and list every clause in §2 with its figure.
2. Compare the scheme: user, GFA (max and min, with JPN 4 groups), SC, height, NBA, parking, DDH, building covenant.
3. For each breach choose a route from §5. Record which are nil-premium and which carry premium.
4. Sequence with planning: s16 / s12A → lease modification → GBP.
5. Run `tools/planning_lease_check.py` to tabulate the checks, the route and an indicative programme and premium.
