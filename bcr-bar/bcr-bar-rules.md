## Cap. 123A – Building (Administration) Regulations

### Plans required for building works (reg 8(1))
| Para | Plan | Must show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (a) | Floor plans and roof plan | Full dimensions, wall thickness, all windows, soil and waste fitments, water tanks, intended use of every part |
| (b) | Elevations and site information | All elevations; levels of adjacent streets to a known datum and to the site and building; width of every adjacent street; any well |
| (ba) | Geotechnical assessment | If the BA requires it, with plans and sections of site formation and foundation works |
| (bb) | Site formation plan | Works and surface-water drainage, survey with contours, geology and groundwater, heavy-rain precautions, sequence and methods, with supporting reports and stability analyses (B(C)R s.21) |
| (bc) | Excavation and lateral support (ELS) plan | If the BA requires it: ELS works, monitoring, sequence, with supporting reports and analyses (B(C)R s.14) |
| (c) | Sections | Foundations, site and lowest floor levels, building height, clear height of every storey, windows, floors and roof of adjoining buildings, max boundary-to-boundary gradient, max excavation depth and fill thickness, volumes of excavation and fill |
| (d) | Foundation plan | Size and position of foundations, site investigation report, grouting; supporting reports in scheduled areas 2 and 4 |
| (e) | Drainage plan | Size and depth of drains and sewers, connections, disconnecting traps, ventilation |
| (f) | Block plan | Building, nearby buildings, neighbouring streets |
| (g) | Key plan | Where the block plan does not locate the site |
| (h) | Diagrammatic plan with calculations | Height, site coverage, plot ratio and disposition to B(P)R regs 19, 20, 21; open space to B(P)R regs 25, 27, 28 (see `../bpr/`) |
| (i) | Structural plans | Structural details and one set of structural calculations |
| (j) | Projections over streets | Canopies, bridges or projections over a street or unleased Government land |
| (k) | Sanitary details | Usable floor area (as defined in Cap. 123I reg 3) and number and type of soil and waste fitments |
| (l) | Ground investigation plan | In scheduled areas |
| (m) | Barrier-free plans | Where B(P)R reg 72 applies, the provision for persons with a disability in detail (see `../bfa/`) |

- Demolition works have their own list (reg 8(3)) and need a stability report (reg 8(4)). Street works: reg 9.
- The BA may require additional plans (reg 10). A foundation plan must go in with the site formation plan (reg 11A).
- Plans are signed by the person who prepared them (reg 12). An AP / RSE / RGE certificate in the specified form goes in with the plans: prepared by them or under their supervision, and to the best of their knowledge compliant with the BO and regulations (reg 18A).

### Scales (reg 13)
| Plan | Not smaller than |
| --- | --- |
| Plans and sections of building works | 1:100 (BA may accept 1:200 for very extensive works) |
| Block plan | 1:500 |
| Street works: key plan / layout / longitudinal horizontal / longitudinal vertical / cross sections / typical road sections | 1:2 500 / 1:200 / 1:500 / 1:200 / 1:200 / 1:50 |

### Statutory periods
| Item | Period | Reg |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approval of plans submitted for the first time (deemed approval under BO s.15) | 60 days from submission | 30(3)(a) |
| Resubmission that the BA treats as a major revision | 60 days from last submission | 30(3)(b) |
| Any other resubmission | 30 days from last submission | 30(3)(c) |
| BA to notify that a resubmission is a major revision | Within 30 days | 30(4) |
| Consent to commence | 28 days | 32(3) |
| Consent for alterations or additions to consented works | 28 days | 33(2) |
| AP's notice of the contractor's appointment and start date (with the contractor's acceptance); TCP appointments where a supervision plan is needed | At least 7 days before commencement | 20(1), 20(2) |
| Registered contractor's completion certificate to the AP | Within 7 days of completion | 25(1) |
| AP's completion certificate to the BA | Within 14 days of completion | 25(2) |
| RSE / RGE certificate to the AP, then AP to the BA | 7 days each | 25(3) |

- Refused plans resubmitted with further particulars count as a fresh application (reg 29(3)). Plans may be submitted and approved separately (regs 29(2), 30(2)).
- Approval is signified by written notice plus stamping, signing and dating of the plans (reg 30(1)).
- Applications use the "specified form". The regulations do not give form numbers (BA5, BA8 and so on): those are in `../submission/forms.json`.

### Duties on site (Part 5)
- The AP supplies copies of approved plans to the registered contractor (reg 36). The registered contractor keeps approved plans and supervision plans on site (reg 40) and supervises the works (reg 41).
- Changes of AP, RSE, RGE or contractor are notified to the BA (reg 22).
- Fees: reg 42 and the Schedule.

## Cap. 123Q – Building (Construction) Regulation
The Regulation states performance requirements; the codes of practice below are the usual deemed-to-satisfy route. The section numbers are from Cap. 123Q; the descriptions are outlines, so read the section before you cite it.

| Subject | Sections | Code of practice usually relied on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Materials | s.3 | Structural codes; material standards they cite |
| Loads | ss.4-12 (dead, imposed, wind, protective barrier, dynamic effects, notices, overloading) | Code of Practice for Dead and Imposed Loads; Wind Effects in Hong Kong 2019 |
| Structural design | ss.13-15 (design methodology, strength and serviceability, stability) | Structural Use of Concrete 2013; Structural Use of Steel 2011; Structural Use of Glass 2018 |
| Construction methods | s.16. Cap. 123A reg 8(1)(bc) requires ELS analyses to demonstrate compliance with s.14 | Geotechnical guidance (GEO) |
| Site investigation and foundations | ss.17-20 | Code of Practice for Foundations 2017 |
| Site formation and retaining walls | ss.21-25 (s.21 is cited in Cap. 123A reg 8(1)(bb)) | Geotechnical guidance (GEO) |
| External walls and curtain walls | ss.26-32 | PNAPs |
| Floors and roofs | ss.33-34 | PNAPs |
| Fire resisting construction | s.35 | FS Code 2011 (2024 Ed.), Parts C, D, E (see `../fs-code/`) |
| Protective barriers | ss.36-38 | PNAPs |
| Lifts and escalators | ss.39-41 | PNAPs |
| Other | s.42 ground treatment, s.43 wells, ss.44-45 chimneys and fireplaces, s.46 vermin, s.47 ducts | PNAPs |

Working rule: when a structural or fire item is checked in this knowledge base, cite the code of practice clause. Compliance with the code is the deemed-to-satisfy route for the matching B(C)R performance requirement.
