## Cap. 123B – Building (Construction) Regulation
The current Regulation is the performance-based version that replaced the old prescriptive B(C)R (commenced end of 2012 – verify). It states performance requirements; the codes of practice below are the usual deemed-to-satisfy route.

| Subject | What the Regulation requires (in outline) | Code of practice usually relied on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Materials | Fit for purpose, to acceptable standards, tested where required | Structural codes; material standards they cite |
| Loads | Dead, imposed and wind loads to be allowed for; imposed load values by use | Code of Practice for Dead and Imposed Loads 2011; Wind Effects in Hong Kong 2019 |
| Structural design | Strength, stability, serviceability, robustness; no disproportionate collapse | Structural Use of Concrete 2013; Structural Use of Steel 2011; Structural Use of Glass 2018 |
| Foundations | Bearing capacity, settlement, effect on adjoining land and buildings | Code of Practice for Foundations 2017 |
| Fire safety | Fire resisting construction, means of access for firefighting, fire properties of elements and materials | FS Code 2011 (2024 Ed.), Parts C, D, E – see `../fs-code/` |
| Other construction | Walls, floors, roofs, drainage of roofs, protective barriers, glass and glazing, curtain walls, windows, chimneys, ventilating systems | PNAPs (e.g. APP-37 protective barriers – verify) |

Working rule: when a structural or fire item is checked in this knowledge base, cite the code of practice clause, and note that compliance with the code is the deemed-to-satisfy route for the corresponding B(C)R performance requirement.

## Cap. 123A – Building (Administration) Regulations
Procedural regulations under the Buildings Ordinance. The submission and approval process (BD plans, consent, OP, minor works, UBW, MBIS) is being covered in a separate thread; this file only records the parts an architect needs when preparing a design.

- **Plans to be submitted:** general building plans, structural plans, drainage plans, site formation plans, etc., each signed by the AP / RSE / RGE as relevant, at prescribed scales (general building plans not smaller than 1:100, site / block plan at a smaller scale – verify exact scales).
- **What general building plans must show:** site and street boundaries and widths, levels, the use of every part, means of escape, fire resisting construction, lighting and ventilation, sanitary fitments, barrier-free facilities, GFA / SC / PR calculations (verify list).
- **Prescribed forms:** BA5 application for approval of plans; BA8 application for consent to commence works; BA10 notice of appointment of AP / RSE / RGE and registered contractors; BA12/BA13/BA14 certificates of completion and application for occupation permit (verify form numbers and titles).
- **Time limits:** the BA's statutory period to approve or refuse plans (60 days for new plans, 30 days for amendments – verify), and for consent to commence (28 days – verify).
- **Supervision:** supervision plans to the Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans 2009 must accompany the application for consent.
