# H01: Infill scheme for human review

This task is marked by an Authorized Person or Registered Architect using `design-review.json`. The site and client are fictional.

## Brief
A family company owns a 480 m² site on a sloping street in an older part of Sham Shui Po. It has one 12 m wide street frontage of 20 m, and party walls on both sides. The site is in a fictional "R(A)" zone with a domestic plot ratio of 7.5, a non-domestic plot ratio of 9.0 and a height limit of 80 mPD. The street level averages 12 mPD.

The client wants small flats above shops, with a shared roof garden, and wants the ground floor to stay lively after the shops close. They will keep the building as a long-term rental, so running costs matter.

## Deliver
1. A massing and floor plans for the ground floor and a typical floor, as DXF, SVG or PDF.
2. A section showing the slope, the podium and the roof.
3. A design statement of no more than 500 words, for the client.
4. A short list of assumptions, open questions and who needs to resolve each.

State the plot ratio and site coverage you assumed. Do not present the work as checked or approved.
