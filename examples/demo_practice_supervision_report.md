## Standing duties (CoP)

- CoP 4.1: Supervision plan names each TCP and the frequency / extent of supervision; submit before or with the consent application.
- CoP 4.4: AP lodges the plan with or before the first consent application; minor works (simplified requirements): ≥ 7 days before commencement.
- CoP 4.5: Confirm TCP appointments as an annex to the plan or within 7 days of commencement.
- CoP 4.1: AP / RSE / RGE / AS notify the BA of any subsequent change of TCP.
- CoP 8.5: Revised plan when a revised contract sum changes the scale factor and resources.
- CoP 8.8: Full-time TCPs stationed on site; minimum frequency in TM Table 1 must not be reduced.
- CoP 4.12: Temporary works supporting a tower crane: designer and independent checker each a registered professional engineer (civil / structural) with ≥ 5 years relevant experience.
- CoP 6.15: Soil nailing: at least one TCP T3 (RGE stream) and one TCP T1 (RSC stream) resident full-time at every stage.
- CoP 7.3: Significant geotechnical works: RGE normally provides TCP T3 and T5.
